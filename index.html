              我做的事，大多是把「电」变成「会动的机器」。
              Most of what I do is turning electricity into a machine that moves.
            

            

              我的兴趣落在硬件和代码的交界处：电源怎么稳、CAN 上丢了帧怎么办、电机低速时为什么抖。这些问题不写进论文，但决定一台机器人能不能跑完一场比赛。
              My interest sits where hardware meets code: how to keep a rail stable, what to do when a CAN frame goes missing, why a motor stutters at low speed. None of it is glamorous, but it decides whether a robot finishes a match.
            

            

              所以我的项目大多是「记录」：把踩过的坑、验证过的电路、终于调通的控制写成能复现的东西。做 RoboMaster 这段时间，我更像一个在实验室里记笔记的人。
              So most of my projects are logs: the pitfalls, the circuits that worked, the control loops that finally ran. RoboMaster has mostly turned me into someone who takes notes in a lab.

            一点想法PHILOSOPHY
            能示波器上看到的现象，就不要靠猜。代码里每一处「应该没问题」，最后都会在赛场上还给你。
            If it can be seen on a scope, it should not be guessed. Every “should be fine” in the code eventually comes back at you on the field.
